            Remote control

            1. Make sure the phone is on the same WiFi (or hotspot) as this computer.
            2. In the Zee-ing Bee Mobile app, tap Scan desktop QR (or scan with the phone's camera to use it in a browser).
            3. Tap the notification to open the remote.
