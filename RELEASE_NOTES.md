# Zee-ing Bee v1.0.10

## Downloads

- **Windows:** x64 `.exe` installer.
- **macOS — Apple Silicon:** arm64 `.dmg` for M-series Macs.
- **macOS — Intel:** x64 `.dmg` for Intel Macs.
- SHA-256 checksum files are included alongside the installers.
- **Android:** `Zee-ing-Bee-Mobile-v1.0.10.apk` on this release. It installs over v1.0.9 and keeps your downloaded songs.

## New

- **Phones download from the laptop.** A phone connected to the desktop now copies the songs, sound banks and background videos from it over WiFi in the background, instead of from the internet. Many phones can do this at once. If the laptop goes away, or is still downloading its own songs, the phone uses the internet as before.
- **Background videos everywhere.** When a song has no music video, or its video won't play, the desktop shows a random background video. The desktop downloads them on its own.
- **More instruments.** The full desktop install adds the Classic and Platinum Major sound banks. Connected phones copy Classic (Platinum Major is too large for a phone).

## Changed

- **Phone:** the play controls (previous, pause, next, stop) and "Now playing" sit right under the search bar, so there's no scrolling to reach them.

## Fixed

- The Support QR code shows on the desktop again.
