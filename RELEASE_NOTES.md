# Zee-ing Bee v1.0.7

## Downloads

- **Windows:** x64 `.exe` installer.
- **macOS — Apple Silicon:** arm64 `.dmg` for M-series Macs.
- **macOS — Intel:** x64 `.dmg` for Intel Macs.
- SHA-256 checksum files are included alongside the installers.
- **Android:** `Zee-ing-Bee-Mobile-v1.0.7.apk` on this release — sideload it (phone needs "install from unknown sources" allowed). If Android says "App not installed", uninstall the old Zee-ing Bee Mobile first, then install this one.

## New

- **Many phones, one desktop.** Several phones can connect to the desktop at once. Each person sets a name, and the reserve list on the desktop and on every phone shows who reserved each song. Anyone can reserve, remove, skip or go back.
- **Play controls on the phone.** While the desktop is singing, the phone shows ⏮ Previous, ❚❚/▶ Pause and play, ⏭ Next and ■ Stop.
- **Disconnect.** Tap **Disconnect** on the phone to stop being a remote and sing on the phone by itself. Scan the desktop QR again to reconnect.
- **The phone tells you about updates.** When a newer version is out, the phone app shows a banner with a download link.

## Changed

- **Search comes first.** On the desktop, just start typing on the song list and it goes into the search box (song numbers work there too). On the phone, the search is now at the top, above the reserve list and the keypad.
- A desktop with the songs built in ("full" install) now also gets the regular app updates. It then downloads the songs in the background, like the app-only install.

## Fixed

- The Windows app and installer showed the default Electron icon. They now use the Zee-ing Bee icon.
