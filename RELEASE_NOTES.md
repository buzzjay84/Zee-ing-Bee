# Zee-ing Bee v1.0.9

## Downloads

- **Windows:** x64 `.exe` installer.
- **macOS — Apple Silicon:** arm64 `.dmg` for M-series Macs.
- **macOS — Intel:** x64 `.dmg` for Intel Macs.
- SHA-256 checksum files are included alongside the installers.
- **Android:** `Zee-ing-Bee-Mobile-v1.0.9.apk` on this release — sideload it (phone needs "install from unknown sources" allowed). **One last time**, uninstall the old Zee-ing Bee Mobile before installing this one. From now on every update installs over the old app and keeps your downloaded songs.

## New

- **A new Settings.** Settings is now a searchable list — Sound, Singing, Lyrics, Your songs and sounds, TV and remote, and About — on the desktop and the phone.
- **Sound mixer.** Bass, drums, the rest of the band, low tone, treble, echo and volume, with − and + buttons, in Settings and under Mixer on the stage. It is remembered.
- **Instruments.** Pick the sound bank every song plays with and hear a short sample.
- **The phone controls the desktop's sound.** While connected, the phone's mixer and melody switch change the desktop's; on its own it keeps its own settings.
- **Who reserved the song.** "Reserved by" shows under the title, and the title, singer and name stay in a corner for the whole song.
- **Better music videos.** 1080p first, newer or live performances over old music videos, Tower Sessions included, and no TV or radio uploads with a station logo. Optional: prefer live performances.
- **Phone:** karaoke videos now show their picture, background videos play behind the lyrics, landscape follows auto-rotate, the intro countdown and count-in dots, and an **Update now** button.
- The version is shown in the window title and under the phone's logo.

## Changed

- The title card stays up through the intro and fades 2 seconds before the singing starts.
- **Restart & update** installs silently and reopens the app.
- Videos fill the screen instead of showing black bars.
- The search box is half as wide on the desktop.
- Reserved songs survive closing the app.

## Fixed

- **Song numbers.** Every built-in song now keeps the number in its pack file, so numbers stay the same and never go past five digits. Numbers change once with this update — print a new songbook.
- The songbook PDF is made again for big libraries, with progress and error messages.
- Typing in search no longer lags.
- Phone updates no longer need an uninstall that deleted the downloaded songs.
- Settings saved on every tap no longer make the app stutter.
- Enter in the settings search no longer closes Settings.
